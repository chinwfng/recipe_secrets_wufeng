Our Story

Three generations of family recipes, one table at a time.

Since 1962

From a Home Kitchen to Your Table

Recipe Secrets began in 1962 in the home kitchen of Rosa Alvarez, who cooked for anyone who walked through her door. Her recipes — written on index cards stained with decades of use — became the foundation of the restaurant her grandchildren opened in 1988.

We still cook from those same cards today. Every braise, every dough, every sauce follows the method Rosa taught her children by hand, long before anyone wrote it down. What makes our recipes "secret" isn't a hidden ingredient — it's the time, technique, and patience that can't be rushed.
